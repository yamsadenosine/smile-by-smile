About me

Hi, I'm Yammin Aldeib, founder of Smile by Smile

I founded Smile by Smile in the spring of 2025. After meeting people who didn't know what a dentist was, I was pushed to change that. Nobody should go their whole life without seeing a dentist, living with pain and hiding their smile, just because of where they were born.

"The best way to find yourself is to lose yourself in the service of others." Mahatma Gandhi
Support the Mission

On the path to dentistry

I'm on the journey toward a career in dentistry. Every hour in the lab, every rubber dam and every prep, is training for the work Smile by Smile exists to do.

A life of service is the most fulfilling life I know. Being able to use what I learn to give someone back their smile is what makes the long hours meaningful.

"Knowledge gives life to nations." Mohammed Fouzi Aldeib
