About Me

Hi, I'm Yammin Aldeib — Founder of Smile by Smile

I founded Smile by Smile in the spring of 2025. After meeting individuals who didn't know what a dentist was, I was pushed to change that. I believe that nobody should go their whole life having never seen a dentist, living with pain and hiding their smile, just because of where they were born.

"The best way to find yourself is to lose yourself in the service of others." — Gandhi

Being the reason someone is able to eat the foods they love, smile at the people they love, and get another shot at happiness — that's the highest honor I could ask for.
